The problem

You sign a year's lease
from six thousand miles away

Every August, thousands of incoming students pick a New Haven apartment from an advertisement, in a second language, in a rental system nobody explained to them.

The lease everyone signs starts 1 June. The academic year starts in late August. The default is paying for two months of an empty apartment, and most people find out after signing.

Impact

What it changes, and what I can prove

Measured

  • Utility burden ranges $0–$579/mo across 133 tracts — U.S. Census ACS
  • Advertised rent understated monthly cost by 14% on the worked example
  • 100% of 152 listings state no deposit
  • Analysis costs $0.001894

Projected

  • One avoided mistake across 20 listings is worth $1,300–$4,400 to that student
  • Serving them costs $0.04
No real user has run it end to end yet. The test that would settle it — prediction against what students actually paid — is the next step, not a finished one.
